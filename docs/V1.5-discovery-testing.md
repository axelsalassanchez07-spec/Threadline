# THREADLINE V1.5 — Outfit Discovery & Rated Look Archive

## Why this change exists
V1.4 had only **13 hard-coded retailer items** and 4 preset `SHOP_SEEDS` (utility, scholar, sporty, premium). A generator branch explicitly favored 3 familiar seeds and repeated combinations even after rating. The budget slider modified the limit but mostly triggered swaps within those same formulas.

## V1.5 improvements
- Generate diverse 3/4-piece combinations from *all* available catalog products and the user's actual wardrobe (top + bottom + footwear, optional outerwear). No fixed trio of preferred style labels.
- Include budget in **initial generation** and change the suggested look when the budget slider changes. Owned items have zero amount to buy. If no unrated look fits the budget, use the lowest-cost unrated alternative and accurately say the limit could not be met.
- Give user taste signals a **gentle** influence: Love increases related suggestions, Skip reduces similar combinations, Maybe remains a weak positive signal. Continue exploring broad combinations.
- Once a look is rated Love / Maybe / Skip, archive the exact piece combination and move on. An archived exact signature is excluded from automatic generation, including Skipped and Loved looks; any archived look can still be reopened intentionally.
- Rated looks are visible under both **Saved Outfits** and **Shop Real Looks**, separate from manually saved shopping looks.
- Save exact generated names, formulas, items and estimated prices when explicitly saving shopping looks.
- Sync `ratings`, `customLook`, `recent`, `selected`, and `generation` through existing Supabase user-owned `shoppingV11` JSON state. No new SQL migration required.

## Safe preview — local guest data only
https://axelsalassanchez07-spec.github.io/Threadline/v1-5-preview/

The preview deliberately omits sign-in and cloud syncing and uses a separate browser database/localStorage namespace. Main production V1.4 remains unchanged. To test:
1. Open the preview and visit **Shop Real Looks** or **Outfit Studio → Discover**.
2. Generate several looks and check they are not simply cycling the old preset seed names.
3. Set the budget to $250, generate 5-10 looks and confirm the price to buy is <= $250.
4. Set the budget to $75. With 13 base products and no owned clothes, the cheapest 3-piece look currently exceeds $75; the app should clearly explain that it cannot meet this constraint rather than claim it did.
5. Rate Love, Maybe, and Skip. Each should appear under **Outfit archive** and in **Saved Outfits**; subsequent automatic suggestions must not repeat that exact combination.
6. Reopen an archived look to view its original items.
7. Save a generated discovery as a shopping look, then reopen it and confirm the exact items and title remain.
8. Refresh and check that the rated archive and recently generated look remain saved in the preview browser.

## Known limitations and future work
- `SHOP_PRODUCTS` remains a manually curated **13-product fixed catalog**. Suggestions are fresh **combinations of existing items**, not live inventory retrieval or entirely new retail products. A larger catalog and actual search/feeds are needed for more types and brands.
- Budget is applied to catalog reference prices, without taxes, delivery, discounts, or live availability. Prices can be outdated.
- Inspiration image analysis still uses written tags, not automatic computer vision.
- The current closet-only studio has a separate generator and save flow; this upgrade primarily targets the Discover/Shop generator and its Love/Maybe/Skip buttons.
- The rated-look archive stores outfit references and notes, not copies of full retailer images. Those images are loaded from retailer URLs and might change or disappear.
